Exercise demo videos

Add a MuscleWiki API key to show real looping exercise demos inside each exercise, instead of leaving the app. The video appears at the top of an exercise when you open it.

Get a key at api.musclewiki.com/register. Direct video access needs the TESTING tier ($10/mo) — the free BASIC tier is playground-only and won't stream video. Without a key, the app shows real start/finish demo photos inside each exercise instead. Your key is stored only on this device.

Calorie lookups with Claude

Add a Claude API key and you can type any meal in the Calories tab, like "2 x KFC Original Twister without garnish or cheese" or "100g droëwors", and Claude works out the calories, protein and fat for you.

Get a key at console.anthropic.com (Settings › API keys) and add some credit; it's pay as you go. Each lookup costs a few US cents, a bit more when it has to search for a brand's nutrition info. Your key is stored only on this phone and isn't included in exports.
Back up your data. Your weekly plans, training logs, workout history, food log and weight history are stored only on this phone — not in the cloud. If you clear Safari's data or change phones, they're gone. Export below and keep the text somewhere safe (a note, or email it to yourself).
Tip: after building your week, export once and keep the text safe. Re-import any time.
